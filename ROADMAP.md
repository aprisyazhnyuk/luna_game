# Luna's next little adventures

## Agreed direction

Portrait, one-handed Android play. Minimal black-and-white pixel art, with a lean, white-and-gray Luna and matching pixel obstacles. Cozy and forgiving, with some challenge in later levels. Hosted on GitHub Pages, installable on the home screen, playable offline after the initial download.

## 1 · Make the first hop feel right (current prototype)

Endless run, one jump, tiny treats, three chances, personal best, original pixel Luna, offline shell. Review jump timing, scale/readability on a real phone, and whether Luna's likeness feels right before expanding.

## 2 · Little destinations

Introduce short levels with endings. Increase difficulty gently with obstacle arrangements, not abrupt speed changes. Teach double jump in a later level with a safe practice section. Decide together whether levels use chances, checkpoints, or unlimited retries.

## 3 · Luna's dress-up drawer

Implemented: single-run treat milestones unlock a bowler hat at 30, a star mark at 45, and adventure boots at 60. The store previews independent hat/mark/shoe combinations and saves unlocks and selections locally with a versioned wardrobe profile. Cosmetics follow the head, torso, and individual paws, with unchanged collision bounds. Next: more items and player feedback on milestone difficulty.

## Later decisions

Sound / mute, more environments, additional outfits, language options, final name, public code license, and an export/import save option. Reference photos guide Luna's artwork and stay private unless the owner chooses otherwise.
