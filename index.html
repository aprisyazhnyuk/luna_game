How to play

Tap to jump. Avoid boxes and yarn. Collect the dots. You get three chances.

Keyboard: Space or ↑ to jump; P or Escape to pause.

Visit the store to dress Luna. In one run, collect 30 treats for a hat, 45 for a new mark, or 60 for shoes. Mix your unlocked items for your next run.

On Android, use Chrome’s Install app / Add to Home screen menu. Wait for the offline status below before disconnecting.
