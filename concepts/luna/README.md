## Prompt set

Shared brief: precise-object edit of the supplied Luna PNG. One full-body right-facing cat sprite on a genuinely transparent background. Preserve Luna's identity, monochrome charcoal/gray/off-white palette, coarse stepped pixel-art edges, original stance, body silhouette, four paws, back fur markings, tabby cap and striped raised tail. Change only the face/head and gray flank patch. Replace the patch with one larger round lower lobe joined to two smaller rounded upper ears: exactly three connected lobes, upright, balanced, solid medium gray, clean pixel contours, same location and overall size, with white fur around it. Keep it readable at 86 pixels wide. No features inside the patch, labels, accessories, backgrounds, gradients or extra characters.

1. **Subtle polish:** keep the original head size, silhouette and ears extremely close to the source. Use a small softer vertical dark eye with one off-white highlight, a slightly smaller triangular nose, compact rounded white cheeks, short friendly mouth and understated whiskers. Preserve the alert gentle expression.
2. **Playful companion:** keep the head nearly the same size; soften forehead steps and round the cheek slightly. Use a moderately larger rounded oval eye with one clear off-white highlight, a small neat triangular nose, happy cat-mouth curve and short whiskers. Warm, curious expression, with the original body and paws preserved.
3. **Extra cute:** enlarge only the head about 12 percent and make it rounder, preserving the tabby cap, pointed ears and side profile. Use a small round dark eye with one pixel glint, tiny nose, soft white cheek, slightly shorter muzzle and tiny cheerful mouth. Preserve the adult body, paws and tail. No giant anime eyes, eyelashes, colored blush or accessories.
